# EIN Walkthrough for Non-Residents (No SSN)

Your EIN (Employer Identification Number) is the US tax ID for your LLC. You need it for federal taxes, US bank accounts, and Stripe/PayPal applications. **You do NOT need a Social Security Number (SSN) to get one** — non-residents apply on **Form SS-4 by fax or mail**.

## Before you start

- [ ] Your LLC is already formed (you'll need the filed Articles of Organization and the exact legal LLC name)
- [ ] A "responsible party" is designated (that's you, or whoever controls the LLC)
- [ ] You have a mailing address that can receive IRS correspondence (a US address or your registered agent's address works best)

## The process, step by step

### 1. Download Form SS-4
Get the current **Form SS-4 (Application for Employer Identification Number)** and its instructions from **irs.gov**. Always use the latest revision — the IRS updates the form and fax numbers, so don't use an old PDF.

### 2. Fill it out (key lines for non-residents)
- **Line 7b** — leave the SSN/ITIN/EIN field blank (you don't have one; that's the whole point)
- **Line 8a** — mark "Foreign" / indicate the responsible party is a foreign person
- **Lines 9a–9b** — entity type: LLC; number of members
- **Line 10** — reason for applying (e.g., "started new business")
- **Line 18** — number of employees expected (0 is fine for most solo founders)
- Sign, date, and add a phone number where the IRS can reach you

### 3. Fax or mail it
Non-residents **cannot** use the IRS online EIN application (it requires an SSN/ITIN). Your options:

- **Fax** — faster; find the current international fax number in the SS-4 instructions
- **Mail** — slower; use the address in the instructions for foreign applicants

> ⚠️ **Fax numbers and mailing addresses change.** Always pull them from the current SS-4 instructions on irs.gov — never from a blog post (including this one).

### 4. Wait
**Timelines vary and the IRS gives no guarantees.** Faxed applications commonly take a few weeks; mailed applications can take 6+ weeks. Plan your launch timeline around this — apply for your EIN the moment your LLC is formed.

### 5. Receive your EIN confirmation letter (CP 575)
Keep this letter safe — banks and payment processors will ask for it.

## Common mistakes

| Mistake | Why it hurts |
|---------|--------------|
| Name mismatch with your Articles of Organization | Rejection or a second, conflicting EIN record |
| Using the online application without an SSN/ITIN | It will fail — non-residents must fax/mail |
| Old fax number from a blog post | Your application goes nowhere |
| Forgetting the responsible party's foreign status | Processing delays and follow-up letters |

## FAQ

**Can I get an EIN before my LLC is formed?**
No — apply after the LLC exists. The IRS application asks for the entity's formation details.

**Do I need an ITIN too?**
Only if you personally (not the LLC) need a US tax ID — e.g., you owe US individual taxes or need one for certain filings. ITINs come from **Form W-7**. Epics Deals handles ITIN applications — [ask us](https://wa.link/x6klxo).
