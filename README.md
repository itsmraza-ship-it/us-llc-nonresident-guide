## ❌ Good News: BOI Is Over

Heads up, because most 2024-era guides get this wrong: **FinCEN's final rule (August 11, 2026) permanently removed Beneficial Ownership Information (BOI) reporting requirements for US companies and US persons.** Your US-formed LLC is exempt. If any guide or provider still lists BOI as a mandatory yearly deadline — they're outdated.
