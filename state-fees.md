# 2026 US LLC State Filing Fees (Verified)

Filing fees only — what the state charges to create your LLC. Verified **October 2026**. Service fees are separate (Epics Deals formation: **$130 + state fee**).

| State | Formation filing fee | Notes |
|-------|---------------------|-------|
| New Mexico | **$50** | Cheapest popular option; no LLC annual report |
| Missouri | **$50** | Low fee; biennial report in some cases |
| Delaware | **$90** | VC/fundraising standard; $300 annual tax due June 1 |
| Wyoming | **$102** | Privacy-friendly; annual report due on anniversary month |
| Florida | **$125** | Annual report due Jan 1 – May 1 ($138.75 if filed late) |
| Texas | **$308** | Higher fee; franchise tax obligations apply |

> ℹ️ **Other states:** fees vary — check the state's Secretary of State / filing office site before filing.

## Important notes

- **These are formation-only fees.** States also charge ongoing annual/biennial report fees or franchise taxes — budget for them (see [tax-deadlines.md](tax-deadlines.md)).
- **Fees change.** States adjust fees; this table was accurate in October 2026. Always verify at the state's official filing office before you file.
- **Expedited processing** costs extra in some states and isn't available everywhere — ask before paying for speed.
- **Don't pick a state on fee alone.** Consider: annual report costs, where your customers/bank are, and (if fundraising) investor expectations.
