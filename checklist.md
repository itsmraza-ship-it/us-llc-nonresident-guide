# The Non-Resident's US LLC Checklist

10 steps from "I have an idea" to a live, compliant US LLC. Do them in order — each unlocks the next.

---

- [ ] **Step 1 — Pick your state**
  Wyoming ($102), New Mexico ($50), and Delaware ($90) are the most popular choices for non-residents. No state is "best" for everyone — Wyoming and New Mexico win on cost and simplicity, Delaware wins if you plan to raise VC money. Full table: [state-fees.md](state-fees.md).

- [ ] **Step 2 — Choose your LLC name**
  Pick something brandable and check it's available in your chosen state (every state has a free online business-name search). Most states require the name to include "LLC" or "Limited Liability Company".

- [ ] **Step 3 — Appoint a registered agent**
  Every state requires a registered agent with a physical address in that state. Formation providers (including Epics Deals) typically include this — don't skip it, or your filing gets rejected.

- [ ] **Step 4 — File your Articles of Organization**
  This is the actual formation filing with the state — the document that creates your LLC. Cost: **$130 + state fee** through [Epics Deals](https://epicsdeals.com). Processing time varies by state (a few days to a few weeks).

- [ ] **Step 5 — Get your EIN (no SSN needed)**
  Your Employer Identification Number is the US tax ID for your LLC — required for taxes, bank accounts, and Stripe/PayPal. Non-residents apply on **Form SS-4 by fax or mail**; timelines vary (often several weeks), and there are no guarantees on IRS processing speed. Full walkthrough: [ein-walkthrough.md](ein-walkthrough.md). **Epics Deals includes EIN free with formation.**

- [ ] **Step 6 — Write your Operating Agreement**
  Not filed with the state, but essential: it defines ownership, profit splits, and decision-making. Banks sometimes ask for it. Templates exist, but get it reviewed if you have co-founders.

- [ ] **Step 7 — Open a US business bank account**
  With your filed Articles + EIN, you can approach US banks and fintechs. **Approval is always subject to the bank's own decision — no provider can guarantee it.** We'll guide you through the options and requirements.

- [ ] **Step 8 — Set up Stripe / PayPal**
  This is the whole point for most founders. With a US LLC + EIN + US bank account, you're eligible to apply for Stripe and PayPal business accounts. **Account approval is subject to each provider's own review** — having a real, compliant US entity is what makes you eligible.

- [ ] **Step 9 — Sort your ITIN (if you need one)**
  If you'll owe US taxes personally or need a US tax ID as an individual (separate from the LLC's EIN), you may need an ITIN via **Form W-7**. Epics Deals handles ITIN applications — ask us on [WhatsApp](https://wa.link/x6klxo).

- [ ] **Step 10 — Stay compliant, every year**
  Your LLC has ongoing obligations: state annual reports/fees and federal tax filings. Miss them and you risk penalties or losing good standing. The deadlines that apply to you: [tax-deadlines.md](tax-deadlines.md).

## 🗑️ What you do NOT need to do

**BOI reporting.** FinCEN's final rule (August 11, 2026) permanently removed BOI requirements for US companies and US persons. Cross it off any old checklist you find.
