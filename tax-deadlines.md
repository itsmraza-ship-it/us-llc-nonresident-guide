# 2026–2027 Tax & Compliance Deadlines for Non-Resident LLC Owners

The deadlines that actually apply to a non-resident-owned US LLC. **No BOI** — FinCEN's final rule (August 11, 2026) permanently removed BOI reporting for US companies and US persons, so any guide still listing BOI as a deadline is outdated.

> ⚠️ This is general information, not tax advice. Your exact filings depend on your LLC's tax election, activity, and treaty country. Confirm with a qualified tax professional.

---

## Federal deadlines

| Deadline | Filing | Who it applies to |
|----------|--------|-------------------|
| **March 15** | Form 1065 (partnership) / Form 1120-S (S corp) | LLCs that elected partnership or S-corp tax status |
| **April 15** | Form 1120 + **Form 5472** | **Foreign-owned single-member LLCs** (treated as disregarded entities) — this is the big one for most non-resident founders |
| **April 15** | Form 1040-NR | Non-resident individuals with US-source income |
| **April 15** | FBAR (FinCEN Form 114) | Anyone with foreign financial accounts totaling >$10k (auto-extension to Oct 15) |
| **June 15** | 2nd-quarter estimated taxes | If you owe estimated tax and live outside the US |

### About Form 5472
If you're a non-resident who owns a US LLC, the IRS generally treats your single-member LLC as a **disregarded entity owned by a foreign person** — which means filing **Form 1120 with Form 5472** reporting related-party transactions. **Penalties for missing or late 5472 filings are significant — don't skip this.** This is the #1 compliance trap for non-resident founders.

## State deadlines (examples — verify for your state)

| State | Obligation | Typical due date |
|-------|-----------|------------------|
| Delaware | LLC annual tax ($300) | June 1 |
| Florida | Annual report | Jan 1 – May 1 |
| Wyoming | Annual report | Anniversary month of formation |
| Texas | Franchise tax report | May 15 |
| New Mexico | — | No LLC annual report |

> Every state is different. **Check your formation state's Secretary of State site** for the current schedule and fees.

## Estimated taxes (if applicable)

If your LLC generates US-taxable income, estimated payments are generally due **April 15, June 15, September 15, and January 15** of the following year.
